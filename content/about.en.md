HardenLab is developing a local Windows hardening package for small businesses and self-employed professionals. The goal is to bring basic security settings together transparently, without requiring Active Directory or central device management.

## Current status

**HardenLab Local Pack is in preparation and not yet available.** This website describes the planned scope and intended license tiers. A launch date has not been set.

## Our approach

- A defined feature set with openly stated limits.
- Local deployment with preparation, a pilot device and functional checks.
- Documented recovery rather than unclear system changes.
- No promise of complete security or universal compatibility.
