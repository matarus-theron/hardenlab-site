title: Frequently asked questions
description: Answers about availability, prerequisites, licensing and the planned scope of HardenLab Local Pack.
---

## Can I buy it now?

No. HardenLab Local Pack is still in preparation. Orders, pre-orders and payments are not available. [Prices and tiers](/en/buy/) are a non-binding preview; a launch date has not been set.

## Who is it for?

Exclusively for businesses and self-employed professionals for business use within their own organization. It is intended for small, locally managed Windows environments without Active Directory, Intune or MDM. Consumer purchases are not offered.

## Do I need IT skills?

Yes. Deployment needs local administrator rights and knowledge of backups, recovery and functional testing. The package does not provide ongoing device management. Involve a qualified person if you do not have these skills.

## Which Windows versions are planned?

Windows 11 Pro and Enterprise in 26H2 and 25H2, in de-DE and en-US. Windows Home and Windows Server are outside the intended scope. Final release approval will be documented before launch.

## Can I undo the changes?

The product documentation provides for a backup of affected settings and a local recovery tool. This does not replace a full system backup. Prepare an independent recovery procedure before every deployment. [More about the workflow](/en/get-started/).

## Could printers, NAS or remote access be affected?

Yes. Firewall and network settings in particular can restrict existing functionality. Test your actual environment on a pilot device before including other PCs.

## Are support and updates included?

Final support scope and update periods have not been set. A planned one-time purchase does not mean unlimited support or an ongoing compatibility commitment. Details will be published before launch.

## Is audit or browser hardening included?

Not in the planned core product. Potential separate extensions are not committed deliverables of a purchase.

## Can I use the package on customer systems?

The intended standard licenses cover internal use within your own organization. Customer deployments, managed services and resale require a separate written agreement.

## Where can I ask other questions?

Email [info@hardenlab.ch](mailto:info@hardenlab.ch). Please do not send passwords, license keys or confidential logs.
